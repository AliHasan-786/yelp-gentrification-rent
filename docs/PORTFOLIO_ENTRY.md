## The Brunch Index

An interactive data essay testing whether gentrification-coded language in Yelp restaurant reviews precedes next-year ZIP-level rent growth across Indianapolis, Nashville, New Orleans, Philadelphia, and Tampa Bay. I built a deterministic Python pipeline that reconstructs a 30-keyword PCA score, refits preregistered fixed-effects models with HC3 robust inference, validates Census ZIP geometry, and powers a dependency-free choropleth, ZIP drill-down, lexicon explorer, and effect-size explainer. The corrected 1,105-row mapping panel finds a statistically reliable but modest association—about +0.276 percentage points of next-year rent growth per score standard deviation against a 6.70% mean—while clearly separating the COVID-era shock, a non-significant quartile result, and the limits of observational evidence.
