## Yelp Open Dataset

Evidence reviewed:

- [Yelp Open Dataset](https://business.yelp.com/data/resources/open-dataset/),
  accessed 2026-11-09. The page characterizes the dataset as a subset intended
  for educational use and describes the downloadable package.
- [Yelp Dataset Terms of Use](https://s3-media0.fl.yelpcdn.com/assets/srv0/engineering_pages/f64cb2d3efcc/assets/vendor/Dataset_User_Agreement.pdf),
  last updated 2023-07-07 and accessed 2026-11-09. The linked first-party PDF
  governs academic-purpose access and contains the applicable restrictions,
  publication-review provision, term, and termination language.

Supported by the reviewed evidence:

- use is scoped to academic purposes and an ongoing course of study;
- the license described is revocable, non-transferable, and
  non-sublicensable;
- raw Yelp data may not be publicly displayed, distributed, or shared;
- commercial and competitive uses are restricted;
- public academic results involving the data or Yelp brand are subject to
  submission to Yelp for review and approval;
- the stated term is 12 months from download or access, followed by the
  agreement's termination/deletion obligations; and
- rights questions can be directed to `dataset@yelp.com`.

Not established by the repository or available records:

- the original download/access date;
- the accepting person or legal entity and that person's authority;
- whether an applicable 12-month term is currently active;
- whether the accepted live artifact or this proposed extension was submitted
  for and received the required Yelp review/approval; or
- whether portfolio publication fits an academic-publication exception.

The Apache-2.0 license on example code, and the public visibility of this
repository, do not transfer or sublicense the dataset rights. No raw review
text, user/business identifiers, or source rows are authorized for a new
public artifact by this record.

## Zillow Observed Rent Index

Evidence reviewed:

- [Zillow Housing Data](https://www.zillow.com/research/data/), accessed
  2026-11-09. The page offers Zillow indices for use in a researcher's own
  analyses and identifies ZORI as a repeat-rent, weighted index.
- [ZORI methodology](https://www.zillow.com/research/methodology-zori-repeat-rent/),
  accessed 2026-11-09. This is the first-party methodological description of
  the rent index.
- [Zillow Terms of Use](https://www.zillow.com/corporate/terms-of-use/), updated
  2025-10-28 and accessed 2026-11-09. Its Local Information terms describe
  limited display of defined Aggregate Data and an attribution requirement;
  public display of other Zillow data requires prior written approval.

Supported by the reviewed evidence:

- Zillow's data page contemplates use of its indices in a researcher's own
  analyses; and
- if the intended display is within the generic terms' defined Local
  Information Aggregate Data permission, the required attribution includes
  “Data Provided by Zillow Group.”

Not established by the reviewed evidence:

- that downloaded ZORI files fall within the generic terms' defined Local
  Information Aggregate Data category;
- that the contemplated public interactive, derived metrics, or redistribution
  are permitted by product-specific ZORI terms; or
- whether prior written approval is required for the intended artifact.

The public data and methodology pages did not expose a product-specific ZORI
license or attribution rule that resolves those questions. A repository-wide
license cannot supply missing source-data rights.

## Decision and required evidence

The scientific protocol is deterministic and ready for an execution reviewer.
The rights gate remains closed. Before outcome execution or any new public
derivative, the responsible source-rights owner must record:

1. the Yelp acceptance/download date, accepting entity, active term, and
   authority to conduct the study;
2. evidence of any Yelp submission and approval required for the intended
   public result;
3. the product-specific terms or written permission governing the ZORI vintage
   and intended public display; and
4. the exact source attribution and deletion/retention obligations that the
   final artifact must implement.

If that evidence cannot be produced, do not execute the outcome study for
public release, do not publish a new derivative, and do not imply that this
record grants permission. Preserve the accepted live release unchanged while
the question is routed to the source owner or an authorized institutional
reviewer.
