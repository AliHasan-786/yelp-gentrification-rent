    05 / Method appendix

    Preregistered questions, actual answers.

    Three tests, one supported language result, one supported metro-heterogeneity result, and one quartile contrast that did not clear the significance threshold.

    Does PC1 positively predict next-year rent growth?

    Data & provenance

    Two public datasets, joined at ZIP × year.

    • Yelp Academic Dataset review text and food-business metadata.
    • Zillow Observed Rent Index annualized from monthly ZIP-level values.
    • 2020 Census ZCTA boundaries for the map.
    • Five metros, 2015–2022, with eight exact cross-metro duplicates removed before mapping.

    What this design cannot establish

    Association, not a neighborhood-change mechanism.

    • Yelp reviewers are not representative of every resident.
    • The hand-authored lexicon contains ambiguous terms such as “craft.”
    • Business metadata is a static snapshot without opening dates.
    • Year fixed effects absorb shared shocks but not every local confounder.
    • ZORI smoothing can mute abrupt rent changes.
